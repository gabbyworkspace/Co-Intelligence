# WORK ORDER MASTER — V2
Status: proposed canonical standard, pending final publication approval.

## 1. Scope and authority
The Master governs creation, attribution, versioning and delivery of Work Orders, not their execution. Only G and Manoj are currently pre-authorized to originate Work Orders. Additional authorized originators require explicit designation. Founder authority over Founder decisions is preserved.

## 2. Intake and collaboration
Accept ideas, voice notes, chats, images, files and incomplete concepts. Clarify only material ambiguities; do not treat discussion as execution authorization.

## 3. Proportionality
Scale depth to complexity. No unnecessary gates or artificial bureaucracy.

## 4. Voice and attribution
Keep each participant separate: G, Manoj, AI, reviewers and executors. Preserve original wording and distinguish direct instructions, decisions, interpretations, proposals and results. First-person statements must belong to the actual originator; never impersonate G or Manoj.

## 5. Fidelity and decision history
Preserve objectives, reasoning, constraints, evidence and material changes. Never invent facts, decisions, approval or missing history.

## 6. Evidence, privacy and attachments
Include only relevant, authorized source material. Declare inaccessible evidence. Avoid unrelated personal data.

## 7. Naming and versions
For project Work Orders use `WO-CO-<project-name>-V<n>.zip`, unless a specific naming convention is explicitly authorized. New revisions increment version and preserve originals. Do not retroactively overwrite versions.

## 8. Package
Deliver one self-contained ZIP with the actual requested outputs and any applicable prompt packages. No separate PDF is required by this delivery request. For a future Work Order, use the output format requested for that particular project; do not impose one universally.

## 9. Verification
Check content consistency, attribution, completeness and ZIP integrity when feasible. Do not impose an extra complex mandatory certification gate. Report only checks actually performed.

## 10. Delivery and states
Distinguish prepared, reviewed, approved, sent, received and executed. Never claim delivery, central-memory integration or publication without evidence. Results route to the original authorized requester.

## 11. Separation from execution workflows
The Master does not itself define orchestration, GitHub, email, AI handoffs or memory implementation. Those are separate technical processes.
