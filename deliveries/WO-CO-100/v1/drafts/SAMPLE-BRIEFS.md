### B1 — UAE regulator puts financial-crime prevention first
**AR:** هيئة تنظيم الألعاب التجارية: مكافحة الجرائم المالية أولوية رقابية

The GCGRA says that preventing financial crime, sharing information internationally, and building supervision in from the start are central to its work. It said this after taking part in the IAGR 2026 conference in Lima this month.

**Why it matters:** expect AML rules to shape every new UAE licence, for operators and suppliers alike.

**Sources:** S15. **Status:** SC-1. Confirm against the GCGRA's own news page before publishing.

### B2 — Wynn Al Marjan Island now opening September 2027, budget up ~$600m
**AR:** افتتاح «وين المرجان» في رأس الخيمة يتأجل إلى سبتمبر 2027

Wynn Resorts has fixed September 2027 as the opening of its Ras Al Khaimah integrated resort, the first licensed gaming facility in the UAE. The opening was previously expected in Q1 2027. Wynn raised the project budget by roughly $600 million, to about $5.7 billion, citing regional conflict effects on supply chains and shipping insurance. Wynn holds 40% of the joint venture.

**Why it matters:** the UAE's land-based gaming market now has a firm start date.

**Sources:** S3, S4, S5. **Status:** SC-2.

### B3 — GCGRA names former Crown Resorts chief Ciarán Carruthers as CEO
**AR:** تعيين سياران كاروثرز رئيساً تنفيذياً لهيئة تنظيم الألعاب التجارية

Carruthers took office on 8 June 2026 as the regulator's second permanent CEO. He succeeds Kevin Mullally, who stepped down in November 2025. Chairman Jim Murren had served as interim CEO in between.

**Why it matters:** the new CEO previously led an operator through a major compliance overhaul.

**Sources:** S6, S7, S36. **Status:** SC-2 / C. Sources differ on the regulator's founding year (2022 vs 2023); use 2023 (S1, S7).

### B4 — Play971 opens legal football betting to UAE residents, but not in Dubai

Play971 is run by Coin Technology Projects LLC and holds the GCGRA's internet gaming and sports wagering licences. It added football markets in June 2026, ahead of the World Cup. Reports say football betting is limited to residents aged 21+ and that access is blocked in Dubai and outside the UAE. **[LEGAL-CHECK]**

**Why it matters:** this is the first GCGRA-licensed online sportsbook in the GCC, with geography and age limits built in.

Note: AcesBook GCC does not link to or recommend any operator. Gambling involves financial risk.

**Sources:** S8 (Dubai/outside-UAE access), S9 (21+ football), S10 (licence types, World Cup markets). **Status:** SC-2. Each sentence now names its source; the earlier "the site states" claim had no source in SOURCES.md and was replaced by the reported fact (S8) (D-9).
**Editorial note:** no link to the operator, no "how to sign up".

### B7 — Posting promotional content in the UAE now requires an Advertiser Permit
**AR:** تصريح «معلن» إلزامي لكل محتوى ترويجي على وسائل التواصل في الإمارات

From 1 February 2026, anyone publishing promotional social media content in the UAE, paid or unpaid, needs a UAE Media Council Advertiser Permit. Fines can reach AED 1 million under Cabinet Resolution 42/2025. **[LEGAL-CHECK]**

**Why it matters:** every influencer post about gaming venues or lottery promotions now falls under media licensing rules.

**Sources:** S16, S17, S18. **Status:** SC-2.
