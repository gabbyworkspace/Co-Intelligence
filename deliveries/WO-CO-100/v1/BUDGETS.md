## 5. Choosing the scenario (G-01, open)

JARVIS does not choose. What separates the three:
- **Ultra-lean:** lowest cash. Depends on two part-time people.
- **Balanced:** adds separate approvers and a compliance reviewer. This makes the C-6 independence real rather than cross-checked.
- **Premium:** adds video and event capacity. It also carries most of the ad-approval risk.

A scenario can be upgraded at a 30-day review without changing the calendar's core slot.
