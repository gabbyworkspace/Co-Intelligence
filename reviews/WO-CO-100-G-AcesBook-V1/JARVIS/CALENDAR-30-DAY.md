# 30-day editorial calendar — JARVIS draft

**Basis:** Zero-spend and Small scenarios, with 1 post per stream per day. Medium and Large add a second daily slot of fresh news.

**Day 1** = the first publishing day after G authorises the launch. No date is set.

**Slots (Dubai time):**
- EN at 07:30
- AR at 08:00
- Breaking news from a primary source is added within 6 h. It does not replace the calendar post.

**Weekly rhythm:**
- Monday: weekly wrap
- Wednesday: explainer
- Friday: light, gaming or tourism
- Saturday or Sunday: low-volume evergreen

Post IDs refer to `POSTS-EN.md` and `POSTS-AR.md`. Video IDs refer to `VIDEO-SCRIPTS.md`. Videos run only from Day 31 unless the Medium or Large scenario is chosen.
